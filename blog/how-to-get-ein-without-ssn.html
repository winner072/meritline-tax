How to Get an EIN Without an SSN: A Guide for Foreign LLC Owners

By Mmesoma Winner, Managing DirectorNovember 1, 20267 min read

Yes, a foreign owner can get an EIN for a U.S. LLC without an SSN or ITIN. The LLC needs its EIN before anything else, because Form 5472 and the pro forma Form 1120 are filed under it, and banks and payment platforms ask for it too. Most foreign owners cannot use the IRS online application, so this guide explains the three routes that do work from abroad, how to complete Form SS-4 correctly for a foreign-owned LLC, and what to do once the number arrives.

Key points

  • No SSN or ITIN is needed. On line 7b of Form SS-4, the IRS instructions say to enter "Foreign" or "N/A".
  • Apply by phone, fax, or mail. The online application is not available to most foreign applicants.
  • Line 9a matters for Form 5472. Check "Other" and write "Foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity-Form 5472".
  • Keep the confirmation letter. The EIN goes on every Form 5472 you file.

Why the EIN comes first

An Employer Identification Number (EIN) is the LLC's U.S. tax number. A foreign-owned single-member LLC needs one even with no employees, because the yearly Form 5472 and pro forma Form 1120 are filed under it: it goes on line 1b of Form 5472 and in item B of the pro forma Form 1120. U.S. banks, Stripe, and marketplaces will also ask for it. Apply as soon as the LLC is formed, so the number is ready long before your first filing deadline.

The three ways to apply from outside the U.S.

The IRS online EIN application is not available if you have no legal residence, principal place of business, or principal office in the United States, and it expects a responsible party with an SSN or ITIN. Foreign owners therefore use one of these routes, all with the same Form SS-4 information:

MethodHowHow long it takes
TelephoneCall the IRS line for international applicants, 267-941-1099 (not toll-free), 6:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Eastern time, Monday to Friday, with your completed Form SS-4 in front of you.The EIN is usually given during the call.
FaxFax the signed Form SS-4 to 304-707-9471 from outside the U.S., and include a fax number for the reply.Generally within 4 business days.
MailInternal Revenue Service, Attn: EIN International Operation, Cincinnati, OH 45999.Approximately 4 weeks.

The person who calls must be authorized to receive the EIN and answer questions about the form. If someone applies for you, such as a tax professional, they are named in the Third Party Designee section of Form SS-4, and their authority ends once the EIN is issued.

Completing Form SS-4 for a foreign-owned LLC

  1. Line 1: legal nameThe LLC's name exactly as it appears on the Articles of Organization or Certificate of Formation, including "LLC".
  2. Lines 4 to 6: addressesA mailing address where the IRS can reach you, which may be abroad, and the county and state where the LLC is registered.
  3. Lines 7a and 7b: responsible partyYour full name as the owner. If you do not have, and are not eligible for, an SSN or ITIN, the instructions say to enter "Foreign" or "N/A" on line 7b. An entry is required, so do not leave it blank.
  4. Lines 8a to 8c: LLC detailsYes, it is an LLC; the number of members is 1; and yes, it was organized in the United States.
  5. Line 9a: type of entityCheck "Other" and write "Foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity-Form 5472", the wording the IRS instructions give for an EIN needed to file Form 5472. Choosing "Partnership" or "Corporation" by mistake can attach the wrong filing requirements to your EIN.
  6. Lines 10 to 12: reason and datesUsually "Started new business", the date the LLC was formed, and December as the closing month of the accounting year for a calendar-year LLC.
  7. Employment and activity linesWith no U.S. employees, enter zero and leave the wage dates blank. Describe the principal activity briefly, such as "consulting" or "e-commerce".
  8. SignatureSign and date the form as the owner, and add a phone and fax number where the IRS can reach you.

After you apply

  • Keep the confirmation letter. The IRS confirms the number in writing, usually on a notice called CP 575. Save it with your company documents; banks often ask for a copy.
  • If you lose it, do not apply again. Ask the IRS for an EIN verification letter (Letter 147C) instead. A second EIN for the same LLC causes problems.
  • Use the number consistently on every Form 5472, pro forma Form 1120, Form 7004, and bank or platform form.

Common EIN mistakes foreign owners make

  • A name that does not match the state record, which can cause bank and IRS mismatches later.
  • The wrong entity type on line 9a, such as "Corporation", which can lead the IRS to expect a full corporate tax return.
  • Leaving line 7b blank instead of writing "Foreign" or "N/A".
  • Applying twice because the first letter was slow to arrive. Wait for the stated processing time, then follow up.
  • Assuming no EIN means no filing. A foreign-owned LLC with reportable transactions must file Form 5472 for every year it existed. If years were missed, see late filings and catch-up.

From EIN to your first Form 5472

Once the EIN is issued, the next filing for most foreign-owned LLCs is Form 5472 with the pro forma Form 1120, due April 15 after the end of the LLC's first tax year. Frequently asked questions

Can I get an EIN without an SSN or ITIN?

Yes. A foreign owner can get an EIN for a U.S. LLC without an SSN or ITIN by applying by phone, fax, or mail with Form SS-4, and entering "Foreign" or "N/A" on line 7b as the IRS instructions describe.

How long does it take to get an EIN from abroad?

By phone on the international line, the EIN is usually given during the call. By fax it generally takes about 4 business days, and by mail approximately 4 weeks.

Why can't I apply for an EIN online?

The IRS online application is not available to applicants with no legal residence, principal place of business, or principal office in the United States, and it expects a responsible party with an SSN or ITIN. Foreign owners use the phone, fax, or mail routes instead.

What do I write on line 9a of Form SS-4 for a foreign-owned LLC?

Check the Other box and write "Foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity-Form 5472", as the IRS instructions direct when the EIN is needed to file Form 5472.

Do I need an ITIN to file Form 5472?

No. Form 5472 is filed under the LLC's EIN. An owner without an SSN or ITIN is identified with a reference ID number and their foreign tax number, or "None".
