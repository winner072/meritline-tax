Form 5472 Deadline 2026 and 2027: Every Date Foreign-Owned LLCs Need

By Mmesoma Winner, Managing DirectorOctober 21, 20265 min read

For most foreign-owned U.S. LLCs, the next Form 5472 deadline is Thursday, April 15, 2027. That return covers the 2026 tax year. The 2026 deadlines for the 2025 tax year have already passed. This page sets out every date in one place and what to do if one has been missed.

Key dates at a glance

  • 2025 tax year: due April 15, 2026, or October 15, 2026 with an extension. Both dates have passed.
  • 2026 tax year: due Thursday, April 15, 2027.
  • Extension: file Form 7004 by April 15, 2027 to move the deadline to Friday, October 15, 2027.
  • Penalty for a late or substantially incomplete Form 5472: $25,000 per form, per year.

The complete Form 5472 calendar

These dates apply to calendar-year, foreign-owned single-member LLCs, which file Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120:

Tax yearOriginal deadlineExtended deadline (Form 7004)Status
2025Wed, April 15, 2026Thu, October 15, 2026Passed: late if not filed
2026Thu, April 15, 2027Fri, October 15, 2027Next deadline
First year: LLC formed in 2026Thu, April 15, 2027Fri, October 15, 2027Covers formation date to December 31, 2026

The rule behind every date is the same: the return is due on the 15th day of the fourth month after the tax year ends, moving to the next business day if that falls on a weekend or federal holiday. If your LLC uses a fiscal year, or was formed part-way through a year, see Form 5472 tax years and due dates.

Where do you stand today?

  • You filed your 2025 return on time. Your next deadline is April 15, 2027. Start gathering 2026 records in January.
  • You formed your LLC in 2026. Your first return is due April 15, 2027, even if the company earned nothing. Paying formation fees or funding the account is usually enough to require a filing.
  • You missed the 2025 deadline. The return is now late, and no further extension is possible. File as soon as possible, with a reasonable cause statement where the facts support one. Read what to do after a missed Form 5472 deadline.
  • You have never filed. Each year since formation is a separate late return. Our catch-up service files all outstanding years together.

Five rules that protect your deadline

  1. Request the extension before April 15Form 7004 must be filed by the original due date. An extension requested on April 16 is not valid.
  2. Fax or mail, never e-fileA foreign-owned LLC cannot e-file this return. It is faxed to the IRS at 855-887-7737 or mailed to Ogden, Utah, under the current instructions.
  3. Keep proof of the date sentSave the fax confirmation or postal receipt. It is your evidence of timely filing.
  4. Use U.S. timeDeadlines run on U.S. dates. For owners in Africa, the Middle East, or Asia, the U.S. deadline arrives hours after it has passed locally, so file early.
  5. Complete every required itemA substantially incomplete Form 5472 is treated as not filed. Accuracy matters as much as timing.

What you need before April 15, 2027

  • The LLC's Articles of Organization and EIN letter
  • Your passport details, home address, and foreign tax number if you have one
  • All 2026 statements for the LLC's bank and payment accounts
  • A dated list of money you put into, and took out of, the company in 2026

Frequently asked questions

What is the Form 5472 deadline for 2026?

For calendar-year foreign-owned LLCs, the 2026 tax year return is due on April 15, 2027, or October 15, 2027 with a timely Form 7004 extension. The deadlines that fell in 2026, April 15 and October 15, applied to the 2025 tax year.

Can I still extend my 2025 return?

No. The last possible date was October 15, 2026, and only for owners who had filed Form 7004 by April 15, 2026. A 2025 return filed now is late.

Does the extension give me more time to pay tax?

A foreign-owned disregarded LLC usually has no tax to pay with this return. Form 7004 extends the time to file the information return.

My LLC had no income in 2026. Is April 15, 2027 still my deadline?

Usually, yes. The filing depends on transactions between you and the company, not on profit.
