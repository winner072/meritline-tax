U.S. LLC Taxes by Country: Form 5472 and Tax Treaty Guides for Foreign Owners

Updated October 26, 202614 guides

Every foreign owner of a single-member U.S. LLC files the same core U.S. return: Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120. What changes from country to country is everything around it: whether your country has an income tax treaty with the United States, how your home tax authority sees the LLC, and the local rules on moving money and reporting foreign assets. These guides cover both sides for 14 countries.

Which countries have a tax treaty with the U.S.?

A tax treaty decides which country may tax certain income and can reduce U.S. withholding. It never removes the Form 5472 filing. Without a treaty, U.S. domestic law applies on its own.

What is the same for every country

  • Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 every year the LLC has transactions with its owner, even with no income. See Form 5472 explained.
  • The same deadline: April 15, or October 15 with Form 7004. See the 2026 and 2027 deadlines.
  • The same penalty: $25,000 per form, per year. See penalty abatement.
  • Form W-8BEN, not W-9, for U.S. platforms and clients.
  • State filings in the state where the LLC was formed.

What changes by country

Treaty access, how your home country taxes and records the LLC, foreign exchange and remittance rules, and local filing dates. Expatriates should also check the rules of the country they are a citizen of, as explained in the Kuwait guide.

Frequently asked questions

Does a tax treaty remove the Form 5472 filing?

No. A treaty decides which country may tax certain income and can reduce U.S. withholding. Form 5472 is an information return, and a foreign-owned U.S. LLC files it every year it has reportable transactions, whether or not a treaty applies.

Does the United States have a tax treaty with Nigeria, Ghana, or Kenya?

No. The United States has no income tax treaty with Nigeria, Ghana, or Kenya, so U.S. tax for their residents follows U.S. domestic law. It does have treaties with South Africa and Egypt.

Is there a U.S. tax treaty with the UAE or Saudi Arabia?

No. The United States has no income tax treaty with the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, or Kuwait. It does have treaties with Turkey, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, and the Philippines.
