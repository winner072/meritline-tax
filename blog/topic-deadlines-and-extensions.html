Form 5472 Deadlines and Extensions: Every Date Foreign-Owned LLCs Need

Updated October 26, 20264 guides

For a calendar-year foreign-owned LLC, the 2026 Form 5472 and pro forma Form 1120 are due on Thursday, April 15, 2027. Filing Form 7004 by that date moves the deadline to Friday, October 15, 2027. Missing either date can bring a $25,000 penalty, so the guides below set out every date and how each one is worked out.

Tax yearOriginal deadlineWith Form 7004
2025April 15, 2026October 15, 2026 (both passed)
2026April 15, 2027October 15, 2027
First year, LLC formed in 2026April 15, 2027October 15, 2027

The rule behind every date

The return is due on the 15th day of the fourth month after the LLC's tax year ends, moving to the next business day if that falls on a weekend or holiday. An extension must be filed by the original due date: a foreign-owned LLC faxes or mails Form 7004 to the dedicated IRS unit, not the regular address. State annual reports and franchise taxes keep their own dates.

Frequently asked questions

When is Form 5472 due for 2026?

For calendar-year LLCs, the 2026 Form 5472 with pro forma Form 1120 is due April 15, 2027, or October 15, 2027 with a timely Form 7004 extension.

Can I extend Form 5472 after April 15?

No. Form 7004 must be filed by the original due date. After that, the only option is to file the return as soon as possible, with a reasonable cause statement where the facts support one.
