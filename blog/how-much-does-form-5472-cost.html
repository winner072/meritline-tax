How Much Does Form 5472 Cost? Prices for Foreign-Owned LLCs

By Mmesoma Winner, Managing DirectorNovember 24, 20264 min read

Our Form 5472 filing starts from $200 per tax year for a foreign-owned LLC with no business activity, and from $250 when the LLC had activity during the year. The pro forma Form 1120 is included, and you receive a fixed written quote before any work begins. The IRS charges no fee to file Form 5472. The cost is the preparation work, which depends on the activity in your LLC. This guide shows our prices, what affects them, and what a missed filing can cost.

Key points

  • From $200 for an LLC with no business activity. The pro forma Form 1120 is included at no extra cost.
  • From $250 when the LLC had activity. More transactions mean more records to review and report.
  • A fixed written quote comes first. We confirm your price in writing before any work begins.
  • Reviewed by a qualified CPA or EA. We tell you who is in charge of your return before we file it.
  • A missed filing costs far more. The penalty is $25,000 per form, per year.

What affects your price

Our price depends on the work your LLC needs, not on its size. These points matter most:

  • The number of reportable transactions in the year
  • How many tax years need to be filed
  • Whether there is more than one related party
  • Whether your records are organised and complete

What is not included

Some costs are paid separately and are not part of our fee:

  • IRS filing fee: none, because the IRS does not charge one
  • State fees and any state tax owed, which are paid by you
  • Registered agent fees, which are charged separately

On request, we make your state payment on your behalf.

Who prepares and reviews your return

Your return is prepared by qualified professionals.

A qualified CPA or EA reviews every return, and we tell you who is in charge of your return before we file it.

How it works

  1. Start with a free 20-minute call, where we tell you which filings apply
  2. You receive a fixed written quote
  3. Work begins once you accept the quote and pay a 50% deposit
  4. We help you collect and organise your documents
  5. We prepare and review your return
  6. The balance is due before filing, and we file by fax or mail

The cost of not filing

A late, missing, or substantially incomplete Form 5472 carries a $25,000 penalty per form, per year. If you have missed a year, filing as early as possible generally puts you in the strongest position.

Frequently asked questions

Does the IRS charge a fee to file Form 5472?

No. The IRS charges no filing fee. The cost is the preparation work. A foreign-owned LLC sends Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 by fax or mail.

Is a short first year cheaper?

Our price depends on the activity in your LLC, not on the length of the period. An LLC with no business activity starts from $200.

What is the difference between $200 and $250?

The $200 price is for an LLC with no business activity. From $250 applies when the LLC had activity, because there are more transactions to review and report.

Do I pay anything upfront?

Work begins once you accept the written quote and pay a 50% deposit. The balance is due before filing.

Who reviews my return?

A qualified CPA or EA reviews every return, and we tell you who is in charge before we file it.
