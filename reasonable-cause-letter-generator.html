What the IRS looks for

A Form 5472 penalty can be waived when the failure was due to reasonable cause and not willful neglect. The regulations for this penalty ask for a written statement setting out all the facts relied on, with a declaration that it is made under penalties of perjury. In practice, a strong statement shows four things:

  1. What stopped youThe specific facts, with dates, not general statements. "I was unaware" on its own is rarely enough, so explain why it was reasonable for you not to know, such as advice you relied on.
  2. That you acted with ordinary careWhat a careful business owner in your position did, or tried to do, at the time.
  3. That you fixed it promptlyWhen you learned of the requirement and how quickly you filed every missing year after that.
  4. That it will not happen againThe steps now in place, such as a professional handling filings and a reminder system.

If your LLC is small and has little contact with the United States, read the small corporation rule in our abatement guide before you write your facts. The rule applies only when all four of its points are true.

How to send it

  • Filing late before any IRS notice: attach the signed statement to the late pro forma Form 1120 and Form 5472, and send the package to the dedicated foreign-owned U.S. DE unit by fax to 855-887-7737 or by mail to the Ogden, Utah address.
  • Responding to a penalty notice: send the statement to the address or fax number printed on the notice, by the response date shown, with a copy of the notice and of any returns you have now filed.
  • Penalty already paid: you can ask for a refund on Form 843, Claim for Refund and Request for Abatement. Send the signed statement with it, together with a copy of the notice and copies of your supporting documents. Time limits apply, so act promptly.
  • Keep evidence: attach or keep copies of anything that supports your facts, such as formation agent emails, medical letters, or your filing confirmations.

Common questions

Will this letter remove my penalty?

No letter can guarantee that. The IRS decides each request on its facts. A clear, specific and honest statement, sent on time with the missing returns, gives the request its best chance.

Is "I did not know about Form 5472" a reasonable cause?

On its own it is usually weak. It is stronger when combined with other facts, such as relying on a formation agent or adviser, living outside the U.S. with no U.S. income, and filing every missing year promptly once you found out.

Do I need to sign it under penalties of perjury?

Yes. The regulations for the Form 5472 penalty ask for the reasonable cause statement to include a declaration that it is made under penalties of perjury, so the draft includes one above your signature.

Is what I type saved or sent anywhere?

No. The builder works entirely in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent unless you copy the text and choose to share it.
