How much does it cost to file Form 5472?

With us, Form 5472 with the pro forma Form 1120 starts from $200 for one tax year. Your exact price is fixed in a free written quote before any work starts.

The starting price covers a review of your company and the years that need a filing, every reportable transaction listed from your bank statements, a draft you review with us before filing, filing by fax or mail, a copy of the filed return, the fax or postal confirmation, and a reminder before next year's deadline. Form 7004 extensions, FBAR, reasonable cause statements and state filings are priced separately.

If you prepare and send the return yourself, there are no fees apart from sending the fax. Compare doing it yourself with using a professional.

The penalty for not filing starts at $25,000 per form, per year. Estimate your penalty.
