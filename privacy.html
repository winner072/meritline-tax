Privacy Policy

How Meritline Tax collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Meritline Tax Services LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company ("Meritline Tax", "we", "us"), collects, uses, and protects the personal information you share with us when you use this website or engage our services.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, country of residence, and details about your business and tax situation submitted through our intake form, by email, or by WhatsApp.
  • Documents you send for an engagement: such as company formation documents, EIN letters, financial records such as bank statements, and identification such as your passport, required to prepare your filings.
  • Basic technical information: such as browser type and pages visited, collected by our hosting provider or analytics tools to keep the website secure and understand how it is used.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide a quote.
  • To prepare and file tax returns, state filings, and other documents you engage us to handle.
  • To send reminders about deadlines and information related to your engagement.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory, and record-keeping obligations.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to deliver our services. When we file on your behalf, we share it with the Internal Revenue Service, state tax agencies, and other government bodies. We also share it with trusted service providers that help us operate, such as email, document storage, and accounting software providers, and the services listed below. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Services this website uses

  • Forms: the quote form, the review form, and the deadline reminder sign-up are sent to us through Formspree, a form service. The quote form also tells us the first page you visited on this website.
  • Booking a call: free calls are booked on Calendly. You enter your booking details on Calendly's website.
  • WhatsApp and email: if you contact us on WhatsApp or by email, your message reaches us through that service.
  • Calendar links: the links on our deadline guides that add a deadline to your calendar open Google Calendar or Outlook, or download a calendar file, with the date filled in. Nothing is sent to us.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to see how the website is used: which pages are visited, which options are chosen in the free tools and the quote form, and which contact links are clicked. It does not receive your name, your email address, or what you type into our forms. We do not use it for advertising.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics does not set cookies. Elsewhere, it sets cookies to count visits. The website sets no other cookies.

The website keeps the first page you visited, the service you picked, and any free tool result you chose to send us in your browser's session storage. The quote form uses them to fill in your request. They are cleared when you close the tab, and they reach us only if you send us your quote request.

Reviews and reminders

  • Reviews: if you send a review and tick the consent box, we may publish it on this website with your first name, and your country if you gave it.
  • Deadline reminders: if you join the free yearly reminder, we use your email address to remind you before your Form 5472 deadline each year. You can ask us to stop at any time.

How we protect your information

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. Please do not send sensitive identification or account numbers through the website contact form; we will provide a secure method when documents are needed.

How long we keep your information

We keep engagement records for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal and professional record-keeping requirements, after which they are securely deleted.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop sending you non-essential messages, by contacting us. Some records may need to be kept to meet legal obligations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this website with its updated date.

Contact us

For privacy questions, contact us at info@meritlinetaxservices.com.

Free yearly deadline reminder

Leave your email and we will remind you before your Form 5472 deadline each year. No charge, and you can ask us to stop at any time.
