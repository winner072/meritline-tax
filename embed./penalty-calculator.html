Maximum possible penalty

$25,000

    The maximum the law allows under section 6038A, not a prediction. It counts $25,000 per form for each year; a tax year that began before 2018 carries $10,000 instead. The IRS decides each case, and reasonable cause relief may reduce or remove it.
