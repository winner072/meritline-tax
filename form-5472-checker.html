What the checker asks, and why

  1. Type of companyForm 5472 applies to U.S. LLCs and corporations, so the first question confirms you have a U.S. entity.

Who usually needs Form 5472?

Most often, a U.S. LLC with a single owner who lives outside the United States. Since 2017, these LLCs file Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 every year they have transactions with their owner, and paying the formation fees or funding the bank account is enough. That is why an LLC with no income usually still files. U.S. corporations that are at least 25% foreign-owned also file it, attached to their Form 1120. For the full rules, read Form 5472 explained.

My LLC had no income. Will the checker still say I need to file?

Usually, yes. Form 5472 depends on transactions between you and the company, such as paying formation fees or funding the bank account, not on whether the LLC made money.

What if the checker shows missed years?

Missed years can be filed late, ideally before the IRS contacts you and with a reasonable cause statement where the facts support one. See how Form 5472 penalty abatement works.
