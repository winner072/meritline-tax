MERITLINE TAX WEBSITE

1. YOUR DETAILS
   Open js/main.js in Notepad. The SETTINGS box at the top holds your email, phone,
   WhatsApp number, business hours and form link. Type them between the quotes and save.
   They appear on every page automatically.

2. PUT IT ONLINE WITH GITHUB PAGES (free)
   - Create a free account at github.com
   - Create a new public repository (for example: meritline-tax)
   - Upload EVERYTHING inside this folder (keep the folders css, js, images, files, blog)
   - In the repository: Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch" > Branch: main > Save
   - Your site is live in a few minutes.

3. KEEP THE FOLDER STRUCTURE
   Do not rename or move files. Pages link to each other by these names.
