Form 5472 Penalty Calculator

Where do things stand today?

Maximum possible penalty

$25,000

    This is the maximum amount the law allows, not a prediction of what the IRS will charge. The IRS decides each case, and relief may reduce or remove the penalty.

    How the penalty is calculated

    The penalty comes from section 6038A of the Internal Revenue Code and applies to foreign-owned single-member LLCs through the Form 5472 rules:

    PartAmountWhen it applies
    Initial penalty$25,000For each Form 5472 that is late, not filed, or substantially incomplete, for each tax year
    Continuation penalty$25,000 per 30 daysIf a failure continues more than 90 days after the IRS notifies you, for each 30-day period, or part of one, that it continues
    MaximumNo capThe continuation penalty keeps adding up until the return is filed

    What can reduce it

    1. Filing before the IRS contacts youThe continuation penalty only starts after an IRS notice, so filing every missing year first keeps it off the table entirely.
    2. A reasonable cause statementA written explanation, filed with the late returns, asking the IRS not to apply the penalty because the delay was not due to neglect.
    3. A timely response to any noticeAn abatement request sent within the deadline on the notice, with supporting evidence.
    4. AppealIf a request is refused, the IRS Independent Office of Appeals can review it.

    Common questions

    Is the result what I will actually pay?

    No. The calculator shows the maximum penalty the law allows for your situation. The IRS may charge less, or nothing, especially where reasonable cause is shown, and it decides each case on its facts.

    Does the penalty apply if my LLC had no income?

    Yes. The Form 5472 penalty is a fixed amount and does not depend on income or tax owed.

    When does the continuation penalty start?

    Only if a failure continues more than 90 days after the IRS notifies you. Then a further $25,000 can apply for each 30-day period, or part of one, until the return is filed.

    Is what I enter saved or sent anywhere?

    No. The calculator works entirely in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent unless you choose to contact us.
