About Meritline Tax

Meritline Tax Services LLC prepares and files Form 5472 with the pro forma Form 1120, state filings, and catch-up returns for people who live outside the United States and own a U.S. LLC.

Compliance for owners who live abroad

Meritline Tax Services LLC, registered in New Mexico, USA, focuses on one group: people outside the United States who own a U.S. LLC. We handle Form 5472 with Pro Forma 1120, FBAR, state annual reports and returns, and catch-up filings for missed years. We also support clients with bookkeeping, LLC formation, and other business and individual returns.

For your Form 5472, you get a written quote before work starts, a draft to review with us before anything is filed, and a copy of the filed return with the fax or postal confirmation. The company is independent and is not affiliated with the IRS or any government agency. We work fully online with owners in any country, by email and WhatsApp.
