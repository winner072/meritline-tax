About Meritline Tax

We help foreign owners of U.S. LLCs stay compliant with the IRS and their state, without the confusion.

What we do

Compliance for owners who live abroad

Meritline Tax focuses on one group: people outside the United States who own a U.S. LLC. We handle Form 5472 with Pro Forma 1120, FBAR, state annual reports and returns, and catch-up filings for missed years. We also support clients with bookkeeping, LLC formation, and other business and individual returns.

Professional. Transparent. Client-focused.We make U.S. tax and business compliance easier to understand, with organized, reliable support for every client.

How we work

  1. Request a free quote

    Tell us about your company, your state, and the tax years that need attention.

  2. Checklist and written quote

    We confirm the filings that apply, send your document checklist, and give you a clear price. A 50% deposit starts the work.

  3. Preparation and filing

    We prepare your filings, review them with you, file them, and track your next deadlines.
